# New Engineer Onboarding

## Day 1
- Collect laptop and hardware key
- Set up 1Password, Slack, Google Workspace
- Read the Information Security Policy and confirm in the HR portal

## Week 1
- Pair with your onboarding buddy for at least 4 hours
- Ship one small change to production. Anything counts — a typo fix is fine. The goal is to walk the deployment path end to end.
- Attend the weekly engineering sync (Thursdays, 14:00 CET)

## Access requests
Production access is not granted during the first 30 days. Request it via the #access channel after your 30-day review, with your manager's approval in the thread.

## Your onboarding buddy
Assigned by your manager before your start date. They are not your manager and nothing you ask them is reported upward.
