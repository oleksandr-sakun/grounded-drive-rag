# Kestrel Labs — Pricing (public)

| Plan | Price | Shipments / month | Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 199 EUR / month | up to 2,000 | S3–S4, email only |
| Growth | 749 EUR / month | up to 25,000 | S2–S4, email + Slack Connect |
| Scale | 2,400 EUR / month | up to 150,000 | S1–S4, 24/7, named CSM |
| Enterprise | custom | unlimited | S1–S4, 24/7, named CSM + solutions engineer |

## Overage
Shipments above the plan limit are billed at 0.02 EUR per shipment on Starter and Growth, and 0.01 EUR on Scale.

## Billing terms
Monthly plans are billed in advance. Annual contracts receive a 15% discount and are invoiced on signature with 30-day payment terms.
All prices exclude VAT.
