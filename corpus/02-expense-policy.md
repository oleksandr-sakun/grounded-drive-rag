# Expense Policy

## What is reimbursable
- Home office equipment, up to 1,200 EUR per employee every 2 years
- Co-working space, up to 250 EUR per month
- Books, courses and conference tickets, up to 1,500 EUR per year
- Client travel, booked through the company travel portal

## What is not reimbursable
- Personal phone plans
- Meals, unless the employee is travelling for a client engagement
- Software subscriptions purchased outside the company procurement process

## Submitting a claim
Submit receipts in Expensify within 60 days of purchase. Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically.
Anything above 500 EUR requires manager approval before the purchase, not after.

## Payout schedule
Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll run, on the 25th.
