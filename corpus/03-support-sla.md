# Customer Support SLA

## Severity definitions
- **S1 — Critical.** Production system down, no workaround. All shipments blocked.
- **S2 — High.** Major feature unavailable, workaround exists.
- **S3 — Normal.** Degraded behaviour, limited impact.
- **S4 — Low.** Question, cosmetic issue, feature request.

## Response targets
| Severity | First response | Update cadence | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 30 minutes | every hour | 8 hours |
| S2 | 2 hours | every 4 hours | 2 business days |
| S3 | 1 business day | daily | 5 business days |
| S4 | 3 business days | on change | best effort |

## Coverage
S1 is covered 24/7. S2–S4 are covered Monday to Friday, 08:00–20:00 CET.

## Escalation
If an S1 is not resolved within 4 hours, the on-call engineer must page the Engineering Manager. If unresolved at 8 hours, it escalates to the VP of Engineering.
