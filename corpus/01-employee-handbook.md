# Kestrel Labs — Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00–16:00 in your local timezone. Outside core hours you manage your own schedule. We do not track hours.

## Paid time off
Every full-time employee receives 25 working days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus public holidays in their country of residence.
Unused days: up to 5 days may be carried into the next year. They expire on 31 March.
Requests of 5 days or more must be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance via the HR portal.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is uncapped and does not require a doctor's note for absences of 3 days or fewer.

## Remote work
Kestrel Labs is remote-first. There is no office attendance requirement. Employees may work from any country where we have a legal entity: Poland, Portugal, Germany, and the United States.
Working from a country outside this list for longer than 30 consecutive days requires written approval from People Ops.
