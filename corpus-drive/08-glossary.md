# Glossary

**Shipment** — a single tracked parcel moving through the Kestrel pipeline. The billing unit.

**Lane** — a configured route between an origin and a destination hub.

**Hub** — a physical sorting facility. Kestrel integrates with 340 hubs across the EU.

**KX-Code** — the internal tracking identifier. Format: `KX-` followed by 10 digits. Not to be confused with the carrier's own tracking number.

**Peak window** — a period of elevated shipment volume, typically Black Friday through late December. Deployment restrictions apply.

**Dead lane** — a lane with no shipments for 90 consecutive days. Automatically archived.
